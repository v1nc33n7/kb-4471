0002 · Duplicates share a cause

owner v1nc33n7 accepted reviewed 2026-10-30

Date: 30 October 2026 · Replaces: 0001 · Fixes: #1, #2

Context

Merging on subject alone put every report into #4471. They hold two causes, and intake tags the cause wrong for some of them (counts: the #4471 split).

Decision

Two reports are the same issue when they share a cause, read from the reporter's tenant (locale and booking form), not from the subject line and not from intake's tag (causeOf in src/lib/kb/causes.ts). Reports with no tenant on file are not merged: read them.

A report the desk reads differently from its cause (a one-day move) stays in its cause's ticket and is footnoted there.

Consequences

  • Slower than subject matching: someone looks up the tenant.
  • One ticket per cause, each with its own count and its own fix.
  • Count by report id: the ticket export repeats reports across pages.
